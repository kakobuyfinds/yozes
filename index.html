HipoBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is a HipoBuy spreadsheet?

It is a product research list you can use to compare options before ordering through a shopping agent. This independent edition links to CAFANS listings. Confirm agent compatibility on the original seller listing.

Does the catalog price include international shipping?

The catalog shows estimated item prices in USD. Get a separate shipping quote for your destination and check the quote for fees, taxes, and any parcel surcharges.

What if the price changes when I choose a size or color?

Use the price shown for the exact variant. Save the variant name and quote date so you can compare it with another listing or check for changes later.
